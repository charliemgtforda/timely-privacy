Privacy Policy

Timely

Timely is designed to help you plan your day with tasks, calendar events, habits, routines, focus sessions, reminders, notifications, iCloud sync, and optional local weather context.

Last updated: June 2, 2026

Information Timely Stores

Timely stores the planning information you add in the app, including tasks, events, notes, routines, habits, focus sessions, preferences, and completion history.

This information is stored on your device. If iCloud is available and enabled for the app, Timely may sync your planner snapshot through your private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit service.

Apple Calendar and Reminders

If you grant permission, Timely can read, create, update, and delete calendar events and reminders so your plans can stay aligned with Apple Calendar and Apple Reminders. Timely only uses this access to provide the planning features you choose to use.

Location and Weather

If you grant location permission, Timely may use your approximate current location to request local weather through Apple's WeatherKit service. Timely does not sell your location information.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, Timely may schedule local reminders for tasks and events. Notification settings can be changed in iOS Settings.

Data Sharing

Timely does not sell your personal information. Timely does not use third-party advertising networks. Data handled by Apple services, including iCloud, Calendar, Reminders, WeatherKit, and notifications, is subject to Apple's privacy practices.

Your Choices

  • You can deny or revoke Calendar, Reminders, Location, and Notification permissions in iOS Settings.
  • You can disable iCloud for Timely in iOS Settings if you do not want app data synced through iCloud.
  • You can delete Timely from your device to remove locally stored app data.

Changes

This policy may be updated when Timely changes. The updated date above will reflect the latest version.
